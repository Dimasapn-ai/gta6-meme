A program-drawn GTA 6 meme for the make.fun "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty. Top panel: a skeleton still waiting for GTA VI since 2013, controller in hand, TV stuck on "… COMING SOON". Bottom panel: the make.fun ghost — sunglasses on, magenta star coin in hand, coins piling up under a Vice City sunset — "PAID AGAIN."

The meme was posted on X by @frmwrkk: https://x.com/frmwrkk/status/2123653740880384415

`meme.mjs` draws `meme.png` (1024×1024) in pure Node standard library — its own raster drawing and PNG encoder, deterministic on every run. `main.ts` copies `POST.json` (the X post's URL) to `/output`, as the bounty requires.
